What Are Cookies?

FunnelSwift.net uses cookies and similar technologies to ensure core functionality, preserve user sessions, and analyze platform traffic.

Types of Cookies Used

Essential Cookies: Required for logging in, account authentication, and security. These cannot be disabled.

Performance & Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with FunnelSwift so we can improve site performance and user experience.

Functional Cookies: Remember your settings such as language or UI preferences.

Managing Cookies

You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences; however, disabling essential cookies may impact your ability to log in or use FunnelSwift properly. By using FunnelSwift, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy.
