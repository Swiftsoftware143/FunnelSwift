Affiliate Guide — How It Works

Welcome to the Affiliate Program!

You send us businesses that need ADA compliance. When they sign up and pay, you earn commission. Simple as that.

The Flow

  1. You share your affiliate link with a business owner
  2. They click the link and land on our signup page
  3. They create their account — we handle everything from there
  4. We track the referral — when they pay, you get paid

Your Affiliate Link

You have a unique affiliate link. When someone signs up through it, the system knows they came from you. Share it however you like:

  • On your website
  • In emails to your clients
  • On social media
  • In person — just tell them your code

What Your Referrals Get

Every business that signs up through your link gets:

  • ✅ Free ADA compliance widget — starts working immediately
  • ✅ 8 pages scanned monthly
  • ✅ Accessibility profiles — Visual, Epilepsy, Hearing, Motor, Cognitive
  • ✅ Monthly compliance reports — emailed automatically
  • ✅ Simple embed code — one line of code on their website

How Tags Work

When a lead signs up through your affiliate link, they get tagged automatically. This tag tells the system:

  • Who referred them (you!)
  • What products they're interested in
  • What tier they should be assigned

You don't need to worry about setting tags — the system handles it. Just share your link and we do the rest.

Tips for Success

  • Target businesses that need ADA compliance — law firms, medical practices, real estate, restaurants, local services
  • Explain why they need it — ADA lawsuits are expensive. A widget is cheap insurance.
  • Share your link everywhere — website footer, email signature, social bio
  • Follow up after a month to see how their widget is doing
