Affiliate Guide — How It Works

Welcome to the FunnelSwift Affiliate Program!

FunnelSwift is the affiliate hub for all SwiftSoftware products. When you refer customers who sign up for any of our SaaS tools, you earn commission on every paid subscription.

Products You Can Earn Commission On

Your affiliate link works across all SwiftSoftware services:

  • FunnelSwift — Lead capture, web-to-lead, landing pages, checkout
  • ADA Swift — Accessibility compliance widgets and scanning
  • MissedCall Respondr — Auto-reply to missed calls via SMS
  • WorkflowSwift — Visual workflow automation with n8n
  • IncentiveSwift — Viral campaigns and loyalty rewards
  • MultiDirectory — Multi-tenant directory SaaS
  • CoreSwift CRM — Pipeline and deal management

The Flow

  1. You share your affiliate link with potential customers
  2. They click and land on the relevant product signup page
  3. They create their account — the system tags them as your referral
  4. They upgrade to a paid plan — you earn commission on that transaction

Your Affiliate Link

You get a unique affiliate link. Anyone who signs up through it gets tagged as your referral. Share it anywhere:

  • On your website or blog
  • In email newsletters
  • On social media and YouTube
  • In person — tell them your affiliate code

How Tracking Works

When a lead clicks your link and creates an account, they're automatically tagged with your affiliate ID. From that point:

  • The system knows they came from you
  • All their paid plan upgrades are attributed to your referral
  • Commissions are tracked per-transaction in the FunnelSwift Admin panel

Tips for Success

  • Know the products — understand what each SwiftSoftware product does so you can match it to your audience
  • Target businesses that need automation — agencies, local services, marketing teams, SaaS founders
  • Share your link everywhere — website footer, email signature, social bio, YouTube descriptions
  • Create content — tutorials, reviews, or case studies about the tools you're promoting
