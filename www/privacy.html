Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 6, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how SwiftSoftware ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects personal information when you use FunnelSwift.net.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information: Name, email address, password, and billing details (processed securely via third-party payment processors like Stripe; we do not store full credit card details).

Usage & Analytics Data: IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and interaction metrics to optimize application performance.

Funnel & Subscriber Data: Information collected from your end-users through the funnels or landing pages you build on FunnelSwift.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data to: provide, maintain, and improve the FunnelSwift platform; process payments and manage accounts; send transaction updates and product support messages; detect and prevent fraud; comply with legal obligations.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with service providers (payment processors, hosting), when required by law, or with your explicit consent.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, export, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at support@swiftsoftware.net.
