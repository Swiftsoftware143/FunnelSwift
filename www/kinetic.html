Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about Kinetic Cards? We have answers.

What is a Kinetic Card?

Unlike standard link-in-bio tools that just give you a static, boring list of buttons, Kinetic Cards are highly interactive elements built to convert. They combine multi-step lead capture, CRM synchronization, and customized automation flows directly inside a beautiful, rapid-loading card interface.

Is it really completely free to join?

Yes! Our core entry plan is 100% free with no credit card required to sign up. You can create your account, configure your initial Kinetic Cards, and collect leads instantly. You only choose to upgrade to our premium Kinetic Tier when you need unlimited active funnels, API endpoints, and advanced team features.

How does Kinetic Cards differ from Linktree?

Linktree forces you to pay if you want basic features like custom domains or watermark removal. With Kinetic Cards, you get those features completely free. Furthermore, we offer native multi-step funnels, lead capture mechanics, and backend CRM integration directly inside the app, while Linktree functions primarily as an external routing index.

Can I connect my own custom domain?

Absolutely. We believe you should always own your web traffic. You can map your custom domains directly to your cards on the free plan, ensuring your branding is clean, cohesive, and professional from day one.

What is a Digital Business Card? How is it different from a bio-link?

A digital business card looks and works like a physical business card — but smarter. It displays your name, title, company logo, phone, email, website, and a personal catchphrase in a clean card layout. Perfect for in-person networking: tap to share, the other person saves your contact info, and FunnelSwift captures them as a lead automatically. Unlike a bio-link (which is a list of links) or a mini-page (which is a full landing page), the digital business card is designed to replace the paper card you hand out at events.
