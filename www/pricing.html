How to pay for a paid plan

Every account starts on a free plan — pick Capture Free or Kinetic Free when you sign up. To move to a paid plan, open your ⭐ Upgrade screen (the Upgrade tab in your dashboard) and send the upgrade request: our team switches the workspace and the limits change at once. Need more domains, more seats, or a volume deal? Ask on the same screen and we will quote it.
