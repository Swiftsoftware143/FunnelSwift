What’s new

Every change below is live in your console. Newest first.

21 November 2026

  • The Android app on the download page is current again. The page had been handing out a build from 28 October while newer fixes sat unpublished; it now serves build 1.0.23, which installs over your existing app as an update instead of asking you to replace it.

20 November 2026

  • Feedback you send can now be answered. The team reads and works the feedback queue from the console instead of only reading it.
  • The inbox opens without a second sign-in. The mail-client door on the Inbox card carries your session through.
  • No broken picture. A profile pointer with no image behind it no longer paints a broken image.
  • Honest receipts. A suppressed receipt is no longer reported as sent, so the sentence you read matches what actually happened.
  • More mail reaches real inboxes. Test addresses on the brand domain no longer bounce against the sending domain.

19 November 2026

  • Download my data. Your account export now has a button on the My Account screen.
  • Your funnel wears your brand. The public funnel page carries your workspace’s own logo and colours.
  • AI assistant access. A new AI Tools screen names the endpoint, your key and the exact block to paste into your assistant — read-only by design.
  • Usage beside your allowances. The usage screen publishes the plan’s limits next to your counts.
  • A refused checkout no longer loses the sale. The request is filed, you get a receipt and a plain sentence, and our team is told.
  • Closing a plan request cannot downgrade you. A request closed after you moved higher leaves your plan alone.
  • Reset links are stored hashed. A password-reset token is kept as a digest, never the value that was mailed.

Earlier this month

  • Comparison charts tell the truth. Features a plan does not include read as not included, and paid tiers are named by the name you see on the pricing page.
  • Free-tier claims match the plans table. The website and the product now state the same limits.
