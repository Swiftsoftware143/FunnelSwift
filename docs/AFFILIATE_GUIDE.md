# FunnelSwift Affiliate Guide

> **Who this is for:** FunnelSwift users who have requested affiliate status. This is the plain-language guide — no technical setup. Admins: see the Admin Guide for the technical configuration.

## What is the Affiliate Program?

FunnelSwift's affiliate program pays you a commission for referring people to the Swift software products — FunnelSwift itself, plus CoreSwift, WorkflowSwift, IncentiveSwift, ADASwift, and the rest.

It works through the leads you already run through FunnelSwift:

1. You bring leads through your FunnelSwift account.
2. When a lead gets connected to a Swift product, they receive a **free account** in that product.
3. If that lead later **upgrades to a paid plan** in that product, you earn a commission.
4. You keep earning on that lead **every time they upgrade — forever.** There is no cookie expiry and no time limit.

## Becoming an Affiliate

1. Log in to your normal FunnelSwift account (no separate login).
2. Go to the **Affiliate** section of your portal.
3. Click **Become an Affiliate** and accept the terms.
4. You're **approved automatically** — no manual review.

Once you request affiliate status, your affiliate dashboard unlocks.

## How You Earn

Your payout rate is tied to the plan your own account is on:

| Plan | Payout % |
|---|---|
| Free (Capture / Kinetic) | 20% |
| Starter / Pro | 30% |
| Suite | 40% |
| Agency | 50% |

Upgrade your own plan and your payout rate increases automatically.

## What Counts as a Commission

Every time a lead that came through your account upgrades to a paid plan in any Swift product, you're credited automatically. You don't fill anything out — it happens in the background. A lead can upgrade, downgrade, and upgrade again months later, and you're credited each time.

## Tracking Your Earnings

Open the **Affiliate** section of your portal any time to see your leads and total earnings.
